# Project Purpose

Millennial Forge exists to communicate the transition from fabrication and welding into software engineering, data systems, AI-powered products, and modern digital infrastructure.

The portfolio serves two purposes:

1. Professional Portfolio
2. Personal Brand Platform

The objective is to demonstrate engineering capability, product thinking, problem-solving ability, and project execution through real software experiences.

# Architecture Principles

## 1. Story Before Technology

Narrative drives the experience.

---

## 2. Focus Before Quantity

A small number of strong projects is preferred over many weak projects.

---

## 3. Validation Before Claims

Projects demonstrate capability instead of relying on marketing statements.

---

## 4. Responsiveness Before Polish

Layouts must be stable before advanced motion and visual effects are introduced.

---

## 5. Product Thinking Before Portfolio Thinking

Every section should feel like a product experience rather than a static webpage.

---

# Major Architecture Decisions

### ADR-001

Projects use a featured-project carousel instead of a traditional project grid.

### ADR-002

Live projects open directly from the portfolio.

Project detail pages are deferred to V2.

### ADR-003

The validation system uses capability indicators instead of traditional feature pills.

### ADR-004

Motion is used to guide focus and navigation rather than create spectacle.

### ADR-005

The portfolio is treated as a software product with release cycles, documentation, testing, and retrospectives.

# Known Limitations

These items are intentionally deferred and are not considered unfinished work.

---

# Major Lessons Learned

* Lock layouts before adding animations.
* Build responsiveness during development.
* Deploy sooner.
* Treat tablets as a unique design environment.
* Live software creates more value than unfinished plans.
